04 EXPERIENCES

The world is bigger than you think.

01
IIM Indore

Atharv Ranbhoomi Fest 2026

"The world was bigger than I had imagined."

Visiting IIM Indore for Atharv Ranbhoomi Fest 2026 gave me a real perspective shift. Coming from a relatively small-town background, I hadn't fully realised how large the world of opportunities could be.

I spoke with students who were building startups, entering competitions, and exploring different ideas—some had even won competitions. Talking to them showed me what people my age were doing and what was possible beyond my familiar surroundings.

It left me with a mix of inspiration, curiosity, and a little self-doubt. I came back feeling more confident about exploring new opportunities, while also seeing how much I still have to learn.
